Functional and Usability Requirements

1. Introduction

This document defines the functional and mobile usability requirements for the Student Hostel Booking Mobile Application. The requirements are based on the project scope, identified user needs, personas, research findings, and the required functions of the proposed mobile application.

The application is intended to help university students search for suitable hostel accommodation, compare available options, select and book a room, and track booking and payment status through a single mobile platform.

2. Functional Requirements

FR1: Student Registration

The application shall allow a new student to create an account by providing the required registration information.

FR2: Student Login

The application shall allow registered students to log in and access their hostel booking information.

FR3: View Available Hostels

The application shall allow students to view available hostels and their room listings.

FR4: Search Hostels

The application shall allow students to search for hostel accommodation using relevant search criteria.

FR5: Filter Hostels

The application shall allow students to filter hostel options to narrow down available accommodation choices.

FR6: View Hostel Details

The application shall allow students to view important hostel information, including photographs, location, facilities, and other relevant details.

FR7: View Room Types

The application shall allow students to view the room types available within a selected hostel.

FR8: View Room Prices

The application shall clearly display the price of each available room option.

FR9: View Room Availability

The application shall display the availability of rooms so that students can identify available accommodation options.

FR10: Compare Accommodation Options

The application shall allow students to compare available hostel or room options before making a selection.

FR11: Select a Room

The application shall allow students to select a preferred available room.

FR12: Review Booking Details

The application shall allow students to review relevant booking information before confirming a booking.

FR13: Confirm a Booking

The application shall allow students to confirm their selected hostel room booking.

FR14: Payment

The application shall provide a payment step for the booking process. The prototype represents the payment interaction; actual payment gateway integration is outside the project scope.

FR15: View Payment Status

The application shall allow students to view the payment status associated with their booking.

FR16: View Booking Status

The application shall allow students to view the current status of their hostel booking.

FR17: Booking Confirmation

The application shall provide clear confirmation after a booking has been successfully submitted.

3. Mobile Usability Requirements

UR1: Simple Navigation

The application shall provide simple and predictable navigation between major sections and booking steps.

UR2: Clear Buttons

Interactive buttons shall use clear and understandable labels so that students can easily identify the action they perform.

UR3: Touch-Friendly Controls

Interactive controls shall be appropriately sized and spaced for comfortable smartphone touch interaction.

UR4: Consistent Design

The application shall maintain consistent layouts, labels, navigation patterns, and interaction behaviour across screens.

UR5: Clear Feedback

The application shall provide clear feedback after important actions, particularly booking confirmation and payment-related actions.

UR6: Error Prevention

The application shall help prevent user errors by allowing students to review important booking information before confirmation.

UR7: Easy-to-Read Information

Hostel names, room types, prices, availability, facilities, booking information, and payment status shall be presented clearly and be easy to read on a smartphone.

UR8: Simple Booking Process

The booking process shall follow a clear sequence of steps from room selection through confirmation.

UR9: Clear Status Visibility

Booking and payment status shall be clearly visible so that students can understand the current state of their booking.

UR10: Accessible Hostel Information

Important hostel information such as prices, photographs, location, facilities, and room availability shall be easy to find.

5. Scope Boundary

The requirements support the functions included in the project scope. The prototype does not require real payment gateway integration, actual hostel management operations, university database integration, real-time room allocation, GPS navigation, or full system development and deployment.
