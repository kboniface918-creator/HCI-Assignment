# User Personas

## Persona 1 – Student Looking for Accommodation

**Name:** Brian Otieno

**Age:** 20

**Course / Year:** Bachelor of Information Technology – Second Year

### Background
Brian is searching for affordable accommodation near campus and wants a quick and reliable booking process.

### Goals
- Find affordable accommodation
- Compare hostels
- View room availability
- Book online
- Receive booking confirmation

### Needs
- Hostel prices
- Photos
- Location
- Secure payment
- Booking confirmation

### Frustrations
- Limited hostel information
- Difficult comparisons
- Unclear booking procedures
- No payment confirmation

## Persona 2 – Student Managing Accommodation

**Name:** Faith Wanjiku

**Age:** 23

**Course / Year:** Bachelor of Business Information Technology – Fourth Year

### Background
Faith books accommodation independently and values convenience and accurate information.

### Goals
- Book quickly
- Track booking status
- Confirm payment
- Access booking history

### Needs
- Easy navigation
- Secure payment
- Booking history
- Notifications

### Frustrations
- Slow booking
- Confusing interface
- Payment failures
- Inaccurate room availability

## Persona Summary

| Persona | Primary Goal | Main Frustration |
|---|---|---|
| Brian Otieno | Find and compare hostels | Insufficient hostel information |
