# Problem Statement

University students often face challenges when searching for suitable hostel accommodation due to the lack of a centralized platform that provides accurate and up-to-date information. Important details such as hostel availability, room types, pricing, facilities, booking status, and payment information are often scattered across different sources or obtained through physical visits.

As a result, students spend significant time and effort comparing accommodation options and may struggle to make informed decisions. There is a need for a user-friendly mobile application that enables students to easily search, compare, and book hostel rooms while tracking their booking and payment status in one place.
